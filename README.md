# Armaan Verma — Portfolio

Hey, I'm Armaan, a Computer Science student at Bennett University specializing in Cybersecurity. I'm interested in software development, UI/UX design, and pretty much anything that lets me build something useful or experiment with new ideas.

This is my personal portfolio, where I bring my development and design work together in one place. It showcases the projects I've worked on, the things I've designed, the technologies I use, and a little more about me.

## About the portfolio

I wanted to build a portfolio that feels like me rather than just another website with a bunch of project cards. It's a place to document what I'm working on, share the things I create, and make it easier for people to explore my work.

I work across both development and design, so the portfolio brings those two sides together. From building web applications to experimenting with layouts, typography, and interfaces, I enjoy working on the entire process of turning an idea into something real.

## What's inside?

- **Projects** — A collection of personal projects and experiments, with information about what they do, the technologies behind them, and the ideas that went into building them.
- **Design work** — Graphic design, UI/UX explorations, interface concepts, and other visual work.
- **About me** — More about my background, interests, and what I enjoy working on.
- **Skills and tools** — The programming languages, frameworks, and design tools I use.
- **Resume** — An overview of my education, skills, and experience.
- **Contact** — Links and details for getting in touch with me.

## Tech stack

The portfolio is built using a modern web development stack.

- **React** — For building the interface and reusable components.
- **TypeScript** — For type safety and more maintainable code.
- **Vite** — For a fast development environment and production builds.
- **HTML and CSS** — For structuring and styling the website.

## Design approach

I prefer keeping things simple and letting the content do the talking. The portfolio follows a minimal, editorial visual direction with a black-and-white palette, generous whitespace, and typography that gives the content room to breathe.

The goal was to make the experience feel clean and easy to navigate without adding elements that don't serve a purpose.

## What's next?

This portfolio isn't something I consider finished. I'll keep adding new projects, updating existing work, and making improvements as I learn new things and experiment with different ideas.

There's always something new to build, something to improve, or a different approach worth trying.
